# Personal Notes: FCAJ Meetup #3 – AWS Knowledge Battle

**Date:** June 20, 2026.  
**Format:** In-person.  
**Location:** Floor 26, Bitexco Financial Tower, 02 Hai Trieu, Ben Nghe Ward, Ho Chi Minh City.  
**Format:** Team-based knowledge competition.  

---

### Overview

Completely different from the familiar motif of sitting and listening to a speaker, this FCAJ Meetup was a total "transformation". The entire hall turned into a fiery arena with an **AWS knowledge question tournament** gathering 8 competing teams. This fresh competitive format ignited the learning energy, turning a meetup into an extremely dynamic and fun playground that "loaded" AWS knowledge incredibly effectively.

---

### Tournament Format

The rules were designed in a highly intense **single-elimination format**:

*   **Round 1 (Quarterfinals):** 8 teams drew lots to pair up for direct head-to-head matches — 4 intense battles to find 4 representatives to advance.
*   **Semifinals:** The 4 winning teams continued to "clash" — 2 fiery matches to select the 2 most outstanding teams entering the final.
*   **Final:** Because the previous matches were so passionately contested that time ran out, the dream final between the last 2 teams was **postponed to the following week**.

---

### Question Difficulty

The question set was cleverly crafted by the organizers, tiered from easy to "mind-bending":

*   **Warm-up (Basic):** Pure definitions like what is an AZ (Availability Zone)? What exactly is EC2?
*   **Acceleration (Intermediate):** Digging deep into features, pricing models, and specific use cases of each AWS service.
*   **Finish Line (Advanced):** Presenting real-world case studies forcing teams to analyze and choose the most optimal AWS architectural solution — this is truly the playground for **Solution Architecture** thinking.

---

### What I Accumulated

Directly rubbing shoulders in a competitive event like this helped me "absorb" and remember knowledge thousands of times longer than just sitting passively and listening. A few hard-earned lessons I drew out:

*   **Learning through real combat:** Being cornered, forced to search my memory and apply AWS knowledge instantly under competitive pressure — created an extremely deep memory imprint in my brain, far better than just attending a lecture.
*   **Training Solution Architecture thinking:** Faced with case study questions, requiring "prescribing the exact right solution" with the correct AWS service for each practical business problem, I tasted for the first time how a true Solutions Architect reasons and finalizes a plan.
*   **Catching weaknesses:** Watching how opposing teams (and my own team) stumbled on difficult questions, I immediately pinpointed the knowledge areas that I myself (as well as the community) urgently need to patch up.
