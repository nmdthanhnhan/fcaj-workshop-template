### General Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The environment here is incredibly dynamic and open. The FCAJ crew is always full of energy and ready to support each other to the fullest, even outside of office hours when we're stuck on a bug. The comfortable workspace helps me focus on coding and learning very well. However, if the team could have a few more "team bonding" sessions or light hangouts to connect and understand each other better outside of work, it would be absolutely "perfect".

**2. Support from mentors / team admins**  
The mentors are simply top-notch and dedicated. What I love most is that they always encourage asking questions, but never "spoon-feed" the answers; instead, they let me tinker, make mistakes, and fix the bugs myself. The team admins handle documents and procedures super smoothly, creating the best conditions for me to focus entirely on learning.

**3. Alignment between work and my major**  
Spot on! The dry theories about Operating Systems, Databases, or AI learned at university were fully brought into "real-world combat". Not only did I solidify my foundational knowledge, but I also opened up a whole new horizon regarding the Cloud ecosystem and MLOps.

**4. Opportunities for learning & skill development**  
This internship felt like an "overclocking" bootcamp. I learned how to use awesome project management tools, how to work seamlessly in a team, and how to communicate professionally in a corporate style. Especially, the real-world battle stories from the mentors helped me get a much clearer career orientation path.

**5. Culture & team spirit**  
The company culture is extremely positive: everyone works with high productivity but always maintains a respectful and cheerful attitude. When projects hit the "burning" deadline phase, everyone jumps in to shoulder the burden and support each other regardless of rank. This made me truly feel like an important piece of the team, not just an "intern passing by".

**6. Policies / benefits for interns**  
The allowance policy is very solid, and the flexible hours made it easy for me to juggle my university studies. What I liked most was getting to join the internal training sessions — full of incredibly valuable, practical knowledge.

### Other Questions

*   **What I am most satisfied with:** Being able to directly get my hands dirty, learn for real, and touch modern technology systems instead of just sitting and reading documentation.
*   **Areas for improvement:** I think the program could expand to include some more "mind-bending" practical labs so the next batches of interns have more room to "try-hard" even further.
*   **Would I recommend it to friends?** 100% yes! This is an ideal environment to turn a confused student into someone with a true problem-solving mindset.

### Suggestions & Wishes

*   **Suggestions:** I hope FCAJ can organize more internal Tech Talks or mini-Hackathons so that interns have a playground to compete and present their own architectural solutions.
*   **Wishes:** I am extremely eager and always ready to continue accompanying the First Cloud AI Journey program in upcoming projects and challenges!
